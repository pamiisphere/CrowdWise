Lives inside the THE MALL app

No separate download or sign-up. Shoppers open CrowdWise from the THE MALL app, sign in with the same account, and Green Points go straight into their M Card balance, the same way M Card points work today.

A color for every zone

Every zone on every floor is now colored on a four-step scale, from quiet green to crowded red, on both the map and the zone list. Tap any zone for the wait time and a better time to come.

Simpler to use

Home answers "is it busy right now?" in one line, with four big shortcuts. Five tabs, each with one job: Home, Map, Trip, Rewards, Profile.

Privacy the shopper can see

Profile shows exactly what's shared and lets the shopper switch it off. Stores get counts, never identities.
